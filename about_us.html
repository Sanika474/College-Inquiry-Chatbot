JD College of Engineering and Management (JDCOEM)

Established in 2008 at Nagpur, Maharashtra, JDCOEM is a premier autonomous educational campus spread across 25+ acres on Kalmeshwar Road. With the guiding motto "Education to Eternity", the institute is accredited with NAAC 'A' Grade and affiliated with Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere.
